      To the Creditors of Webooook Information Technology Ltd.:
      On June 19, 2025, Webooook Information Technology Ltd. (“Webooook”) filed a Notice of Intention
      to Make a Proposal (“NOI”) pursuant to the provisions of the Bankruptcy and Insolvency Act
      (“BIA”), and Ernst & Young Inc. was appointed as Proposal Trustee (the “Trustee”).
      Pursuant to the BIA, the effect of filing an NOI is an automatic stay of proceedings, which prohibits
      all creditors from continuing or commencing any actions, execution or proceeding against Webooook
      for an initial stay period of 30 days (the “Stay Period”), although the Court may extend this period.
      You will find enclosed herewith the following documents:
      • a copy of the notice of intention;
      • the certificate of filing of an NOI issued by the Official Receiver; and
      • a preliminary list of creditors with claims of $250 or more.
      During the NOI proceeding, Webooook continues to operate in the ordinary course of business. The
      Trustee is responsible for monitoring Webooook’s business and affairs during the Stay Period and
      reporting any material adverse changes to the Office of the Superintendent of Bankruptcy and the
      Court.
      Webooook intends to seek Court approval for, among other things, a sale and investment solicitation
      process (the “SISP”) as well as an interim financing agreement. The proposed SISP aims to solicit
      interest in an acquisition or refinancing of the business, or a sale of the assets and/or business of
      Webooook, through various means such as merger, reorganization, recapitalization, primary equity
      issuance, or other similar transactions. The proposed deadline to submit a binding offer is July 14,
      2025.
      Documents and information pertinent to the NOI proceeding are available at the Trustee’s case
      website: www.ey.com/ca/webooook . Should you have any general questions, please contact the
      Trustee at:
